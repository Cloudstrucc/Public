═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT'S CHANGING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Starting [DATE], all Microsoft Teams meetings will use **Sensitivity Labels** to enforce appropriate security controls based on meeting content. You will see two new options when creating meetings:

🔴 **PROTECTED B - SECURE MEETING**
   Use for: Classified information, NDA-covered content, sensitive discussions
   
   Security features (LOCKED - cannot be changed):
   • Watermarks on all video feeds and screen shares
   • Lobby restricted to Leonardo Company employees only
   • Only meeting organizer can present
   • External users cannot join
   • Recording controlled by organizer only
   • Content cannot be copied to clipboard
   • End-to-end encryption enabled

🟢 **GENERAL - REGULAR MEETING**
   Use for: Team syncs, project updates, training, casual collaboration
   
   Security features (FLEXIBLE - you control):
   • No watermarks
   • You control lobby settings
   • You control who can present
   • External users can join (if you allow)
   • Standard Teams features available

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY THIS IS CRITICAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

As a defense contractor, Leonardo Company handles:
✓ Classified government contracts
✓ Proprietary defense technologies
✓ Export-controlled information (ITAR/EAR)
✓ Sensitive customer data
✓ Confidential business strategies

**The Risk:**
Without proper controls, a single misconfigured meeting can result in:
❌ Unauthorized recording of classified discussions
❌ External parties accessing sensitive information
❌ Data spillage to unauthorized systems
❌ Non-compliance with federal regulations (DFARS, NIST 800-171)
❌ Contract violations and potential loss of clearance
❌ Legal liability and financial penalties

**Our Cloud Environment:**
While Microsoft 365 provides enterprise-grade security, we must apply additional **guardrails** to ensure our specific requirements are met:

• **Cloud Security Model:** Shared responsibility - Microsoft secures infrastructure, 
  we secure our data and access controls
  
• **Zero Trust Approach:** Never trust, always verify - even internal meetings need 
  appropriate controls
  
• **Defense in Depth:** Multiple layers of security controls prevent accidental or 
  intentional data exposure
  
• **Compliance Requirements:** Federal contracts require documented security controls 
  and audit trails

**What These Labels Do:**
✓ Enforce consistent security standards across all meetings
✓ Prevent accidental exposure of classified information
✓ Provide audit trail for compliance reporting
✓ Protect intellectual property from unauthorized access
✓ Ensure watermarking for meeting attribution and deterrence
✓ Meet DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST 800-171 requirements

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Starting [DATE]:**

1. **Creating Meetings:**
   When you create a new Teams meeting, you'll see a "Sensitivity" dropdown near 
   the meeting title. By default, "Protected B - Secure Meeting" will be selected.

2. **Choosing the Right Label:**
   
   ALWAYS use "Protected B - Secure Meeting" for:
   ✓ Any discussion of classified information
   ✓ Contract details, pricing, or proposals
   ✓ Technical specifications or designs
   ✓ Meetings with government representatives
   ✓ NDA-covered discussions
   ✓ Any meeting you wouldn't want recorded by unauthorized parties
   
   Use "General - Regular Meeting" for:
   ✓ Team stand-ups and status updates (non-sensitive)
   ✓ Training sessions (non-classified content)
   ✓ Social gatherings or casual calls
   ✓ Public presentations or webinars
   ✓ Routine administrative meetings

3. **What You'll Experience:**
   
   In PROTECTED B meetings:
   • You'll see watermarks on everyone's video (your name + timestamp)
   • You'll see watermarks on all screen shares
   • External users cannot join, even if invited
   • Only you (the organizer) can present by default
   • Settings are locked with 🔒 icons in meeting options
   
   In GENERAL meetings:
   • No watermarks
   • Normal Teams functionality
   • You control all meeting options

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Q: What if I forget to select the right label?**
A: Don't worry! Protected B is the default. For casual meetings, just change it to 
   General before sending the invite.

**Q: Can I change the label after creating the meeting?**
A: Yes, you can change it before the meeting starts. After the meeting starts, 
   settings are enforced and cannot be changed.

**Q: Will this affect my existing meetings?**
A: Existing meetings will continue to work as they currently do. New security 
   controls only apply to meetings created after [DATE].

**Q: What if I need to share my screen in a Protected B meeting but I'm not the organizer?**
A: Ask the organizer to promote you to co-organizer. They can do this in meeting 
   options or during the meeting.

**Q: Do watermarks affect meeting quality or performance?**
A: No. Watermarks are lightweight overlays and do not impact video quality or 
   network performance.

**Q: Can external partners see the watermarks?**
A: External users cannot join Protected B meetings. For meetings with partners, 
   use General meetings where appropriate, or conduct discussions in person/via 
   secure channels.

**Q: Are phone dial-in users affected?**
A: Yes. In Protected B meetings, phone dial-in users must wait in the lobby and 
   be admitted by the organizer.

**Q: What happens if I select the wrong label?**
A: If you realize before the meeting:
   - Open meeting options → Change the label → Save
   
   If you realize during the meeting:
   - Continue the meeting with current settings
   - Schedule a new meeting with correct label for sensitive topics
