EC 20010

Nomination Paper of a Candidate at a Federal General Election or By-election

Official candidate nomination form as required under section 66 of the Canada Elections Act for federal elections

Nomination Requirements

This nomination paper must be filed after the issue of the Notice of Election and before the nomination deadline, which is 21 days before election day at 2:00 p.m. local time. The candidate must be a Canadian citizen aged 18 or older on election day.

Key Nomination Requirements

Elector Signatures

100 qualified electors must consent to candidacy (50 in remote districts listed in Schedule 3)

Proof of Identity

Valid identification documents must be provided to the returning officer

Solemn Declaration

Candidate must make a witnessed declaration before an authorized person

Nomination Paper Sections

Section A - Candidate Information MANDATORY

Personal details, electoral district, political affiliation, contact information, occupation, and privacy authorizations.

Section B - Campaign Delegate OPTIONAL

Authorized individual who can submit the nomination paper and supporting documents on behalf of the candidate.

Section C - Official Agent MANDATORY

Person responsible for managing campaign finances and reporting financial transactions to Elections Canada.

Section D - Auditor OPTIONAL

Professional accountant required if campaign contributions or expenses exceed $10,000, or candidate receives 10% of votes.

Section E - Campaign Office OPTIONAL

Campaign contact information and website details to be published on Elections Canada's website.

Section F - Electors Consenting MANDATORY

Signatures and information from qualified electors who consent to the candidate's nomination.

Candidate Eligibility

  • Canadian citizen aged 18+ on election day
  • Qualified as an elector on nomination filing date
  • Not disqualified under section 65 of the Canada Elections Act
  • Can run in only one electoral district at a time

Required Documentation

  • Completed nomination paper with all mandatory sections
  • Proof of identity documents
  • At least 100 elector signatures (150 recommended)
  • Political party endorsement (if applicable)

Important Deadlines

  • File after Notice of Election is issued
  • Submit 21 days before election day by 2:00 p.m.
  • Party endorsement required 24 hours before nomination close
  • Make appointment with returning officer early

Alternative Options

  • Submit physical paper form to returning officer
  • Complete online nomination via PESC portal
  • Use campaign delegate for submission
  • Schedule appointment to ensure timely processing

Electronic Signature & Digital Submission

Legal Electronic Signatures

When you submit this nomination form electronically, you are providing your legal electronic signature. Multiple parties must sign including the candidate, official agent, and auditor (if applicable). These digital signatures carry the same legal weight as handwritten signatures.

Solemn Declaration Process

The candidate's electronic signature constitutes their solemn declaration as required under the Canada Elections Act. This declaration confirms eligibility, consent to nomination, and acknowledgment of responsibilities under federal election law.

Digital Processing

Electronic nominations are processed immediately upon receipt by the returning officer. Confirmation of nomination status will be provided electronically, and all campaign materials can be accessed through the PESC portal.
