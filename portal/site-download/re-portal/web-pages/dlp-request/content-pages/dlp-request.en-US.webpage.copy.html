DLP Policy Process

  1. Submit policy request
  2. Security team reviews requirements
  3. Business stakeholders approve
  4. Policy is configured and tested
  5. Policy goes live in target environments

Review Timeline

  • New Policy: 5-7 business days
  • Modification: 3-5 business days
  • Review: 2-3 business days
  • Exemption: 1-2 weeks

Connector Categories

Business Data
Connectors that can access organizational data (SharePoint, Dynamics, SQL)
Non-Business Data
Connectors for public data only (Weather, News, RSS feeds)
Blocked
Connectors not allowed in the organization (Social media, Consumer services)

Important Notes

  • DLP policies affect all apps and flows in selected environments
  • Test policies in development environments first
  • Policies help ensure compliance with data protection regulations
